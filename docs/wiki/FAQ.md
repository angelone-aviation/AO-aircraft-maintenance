# FAQ

## General

**Is Angel One the official maintenance record?**
No. The signed paper logbook is. Angel One writes the logbook entry wording for
signature and keeps the status records 14 CFR 91.417(a)(2) requires
([Work orders](Work-Orders.md#logbook-entry)).

**Why can't I edit a finished record?**
Finished records are safety records. You add a correction with a reason, and
the original is kept. Every change is in [Change history](Change-History.md).

**Why don't I see a screen my colleague sees?**
Your permission groups decide your menu ([roles](Home.md#roles)). Ask your
Flight Program administrator.

**How do I switch Flight Program?**
Use the selector at the top of the sidebar, or under **All sections** on a
phone. It needs a connection.

**Where do I change my name, email or password?**
Ask a system administrator. There is no self-service reset, and you cannot
change your own password. Owners and board members have no Settings page.

**Is there a test version I can try?**
**Explore the local demo** on the sign-in page, where offered, opens fictional
aircraft stored only in your browser. **Preview owner portal** and **Preview
board view** show those views.

## Flying

**Why can't I change the starting Hobbs or tach?**
They come from the aircraft's totals so every flight joins the last one.
Compare them with the meters. If they differ, tell your administrator or a
mechanic: the aircraft's totals are corrected with **Edit aircraft**, which
the change log records.

**Why is my flight "Needs review"?**
The aircraft changed before your flight arrived, or a reading looks wrong. It
does not count until an administrator reviews it. Nothing is lost
([reasons](Flight-Log.md#flights-held-for-review)).

**Why can't I log a flight?**
The aircraft is grounded ([the dispatch gate](Flight-Log.md#the-dispatch-gate)).

**Can I log two legs offline?**
Yes. The second leg starts where the first ended, and they sync in order.

**Do my checklist checkmarks count as a record?**
No. They are personal and stay on your device.

**What does "Setup incomplete" mean? Can we fly?**
The aircraft's records are still being loaded. It does not ground the aircraft,
so flights can be logged, but it never reads Airworthy.

## Maintenance

**When is something overdue?**
Hours, cycles and landings limits at 0 remaining; calendar limits from the day
after the due date ([details](Overview-and-Aircraft.md#when-a-limit-is-overdue)).

**I completed the job card. Why is the inspection still due?**
Job cards do not record compliance. Close the work order
([Work orders](Work-Orders.md#close-out)).

**I closed the work order. Why is the AD still due, and the problem still open?**
Close-out records inspection compliance only. Use **Record compliance** on the
AD and **Record resolution** on the problem.

**Why can't I record the inspection signoff?**
You need an approved, numbered mechanic certificate, and you must not be the
technician who signed ([Job cards](Job-Cards.md#who-can-sign)).

**How long can a problem stay deferred?**
By MEL category: B 3 days, C 10 days, D 120 days, A as the MEL states, all
counted from the day the defect was found. A 91.213(d) deferral does not
expire ([Discrepancies](Discrepancies.md#defer-a-problem)).

**Can I delete a photo, manual or component?**
No. Photos are archived with a reason; manuals and components are kept.

## Data and devices

**What does "Load older" mean?**
The device keeps recent history (3 or 6 months, depending on the list) and
every open item. **Load older** fetches older records from the server. It
needs a connection ([what the device keeps](Offline.md#what-the-device-keeps)).

**What happens if I sign out with entries not sent?**
The app asks first. **Delete and sign out** deletes them for good; **Stay
signed in** keeps them ([signing out](Offline.md#signing-out)).

**How long can I work offline?**
Seven days from sign-in ([Offline](Offline.md#seven-days)).

**Is my phone's copy safe?**
The copy is not encrypted on the device. Use a screen lock, and sign out of
shared devices.

**The app shows "Configuration error". What do I do?**
Reload the page. If it stays, tell your administrator; your device's data is
safe ([Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md#configuration-error)).

**How do I get everything we hold on one aircraft?**
**Export records package** on **View aircraft**
([Records package](Records-Package-and-Exports.md#records-package)).

**Why did an email arrive at a different time than the bell changed?**
Emails use the program's records time zone; the bell uses your device's date.
