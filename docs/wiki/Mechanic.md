# Mechanic guide

You keep the aircraft's maintenance current: inspections, ADs, component
limits, problem reports, work orders and job cards. The default
**Maintenance technicians** group can read, report and resolve discrepancies
and manage maintenance on its aircraft.

Most maintenance changes need a connection. Records already on the device stay
readable offline ([Offline](Offline.md)).

## What you see

| Group    | Screens                                                                                                                                        |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fly      | Overview, Flight plans, Checklists, Flight log, Discrepancies                                                                                  |
| Maintain | Inspections, Planning, Maintenance calendar, Airworthiness directives, Work orders, Job cards, Parts & repair requests, Components, Scan label |
| Manage   | Flight Programs, Aircraft, Saved addresses, Reference library, Sync center, Change history, Alerts, Glossary, Settings                         |

Inventory, Purchase orders and Vendors are for administrators. Your Overview
opens with the maintenance outlook: what is overdue and due soon.

## A typical job, start to finish

| Step | What you do                                                                    | Where                                                              |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | See what is due                                                                | [Planning and due items](Planning-and-Due-Items.md)                |
| 2    | Open a work order and add the inspection, problem report or AD action          | [Work orders](Work-Orders.md#open-a-work-order)                    |
| 3    | Request parts                                                                  | [Parts requests](Inventory-and-Parts-Requests.md#parts-requests)   |
| 4    | Record the work and sign each job card                                         | [Job cards](Job-Cards.md#complete-and-sign-a-job-card)             |
| 5    | Resolve the problem reports the work fixed                                     | [Discrepancies](Discrepancies.md#resolve-a-problem)                |
| 6    | Record AD compliance                                                           | [Inspections and ADs](Inspections-and-ADs.md#record-ad-compliance) |
| 7    | Close the work order                                                           | [Work orders](Work-Orders.md#close-out)                            |
| 8    | Generate the logbook entry, sign the paper logbook, attach a photo of the page | [Work orders](Work-Orders.md#logbook-entry)                        |

Completing a job card does not update inspection compliance, resolve a
problem or move a component. Closing the work order records inspection
compliance; problems and AD compliance are recorded on their own screens.

## Your certificate

You need an approved, numbered mechanic certificate to record an inspection
signoff.

1. Choose **My certifications** under your name, or **Settings → My account
   → Edit my certifications**.
2. Tick **Certified mechanic** and enter the number, issue date and ratings
   (Airframe, Powerplant, Inspection Authorization).
3. Attach a copy of your certificate if your program asks for one.
4. Save. It reads "Waiting for approval" until a Flight Program administrator
   or system administrator approves it.

See [Job cards](Job-Cards.md#who-can-sign).

## Your regular checks

See the "Daily" and "Weekly" sections of the
[checklists](../handbook/3-running-it/checklists.md).

1. Weekly: **Planning** (next 30 days) and the **Maintenance calendar**.
2. Weekly: open problem reports on **Discrepancies**, and deferrals near their
   expiry.
3. Weekly: open work orders and job cards waiting for a signoff.
4. Whenever an alert arrives: overdue inspections, ADs and component limits
   under the bell ([Alerts](Alerts.md)).
