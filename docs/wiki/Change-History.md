# Change history

**Change history** (under **Manage**) is the change log of the active Flight
Program: every submission, correction and administrative change, newest first.
Each entry says who, when, the reason where one was required, and the values
before and after.

Nothing in the log can be edited or deleted. That is why records are corrected
by appending: the correction and the original both stay here.

## Read the log

1. Open **Change history**. The **Change log** tab is open.
2. Choose an aircraft, or **All aircraft and program records**.
3. Open an entry to see the values before and after.
4. Choose **Load older** at the bottom for older entries.

With an aircraft chosen, **Export records package** saves that aircraft's
complete record ([Records package](Records-Package-and-Exports.md#records-package)).

Most records also have a **Record history** section in their form, listing the
changes to that one record.

## Who sees what

Everyone who can read the program can open Change history. Each person sees
only the entries about records they may see.

| Entries about                                                   | Seen by                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Aircraft and their records                                      | Members who can read the aircraft                   |
| Inventory, stock, vendors, costs, expiring dates                | Flight Program administrators                       |
| The program's groups, members' access, saved addresses          | Flight Program administrators                       |
| Password resets, ended sessions, refused sign-ins, certificates | System administrators, under **Unassigned records** |

After a record moves to another Flight Program, its history moves with it.

## Load older

"Newest changes first · Load older" fetches older entries page by page. It
needs a connection. Offline, the log lists only the recent entries kept on the
device, so some may be missing until you reconnect.
