# Task cards

Use **Task templates → Create task template** on an inspection, installed component service requirement, or discrepancy to prepare reusable instructions. Adding that source to a work order creates independent job cards for the order. Open **Job cards** in the order details to record work and signoffs. Standalone cards can also be created from **Job cards → New job card** (under **Maintain** in the navigation).

Templates contain instructions, notes and required parts; results and signoffs belong to work-order copies. Each copy retains its template origin, starts in draft, and has no execution results. Later template edits apply to future copies. For existing orders, use **Add missing task templates**. Repeating this action does not duplicate existing copies.

Card numbers are unique within the aircraft's current Flight Program. Leave the number blank for `TC-YYYY-NNNN`. The table filters by aircraft and status, and its search matches the card number, title, description, aircraft registration and work order number. Linked records include an optional open work order, inspection, installed component, component service requirement and originating discrepancy. Related records must belong to the same aircraft. A saved work-order association cannot be reassigned.

The editor includes ATA chapter, description, maintenance-data references, applicability and a **Notes / remarks** field. Planning contains only **Required parts**. Previous step data remains readable in **Previously recorded step details**; it is no longer an editable checklist or a signoff prerequisite. Existing planning and functional-test data is retained in storage.

Execution records include findings, actual work, additional measurements, actual labor and completion date. Parts-used rows record part number/description, positive quantity, serial or lot and removed/installed component details. Choose **Non-stock item** for manual entry, or **Inventory stock** and select a storage location and stock item. Stock selection uses your existing inventory access, fills the part number and description and must belong to the aircraft’s Flight Program. Each stock item shows its available quantity (on hand less reservations), as in parts requests. Lots past their shelf life are marked **Expired** and cannot be linked; a lot linked before it expired stays on the card. A part already recorded keeps its part number if the stock item's part number is corrected later; only a newly entered number must match the stock item. Linking records the source; issue quantities separately through **Inventory → Move stock**. Component installation changes also remain separate.

Save a draft before uploading evidence. PDF, JPEG and PNG files up to 7 MB can be categorized as photos, supporting documents or parts traceability. Evidence is stored with the card and remains readable after completion.

Statuses are draft, ready, in progress, awaiting parts, awaiting inspection, completed and canceled. To complete a card:

1. Change Draft to In progress, enter actual work performed and a completion date, then save.
2. Record technician signoff, then inspection signoff when required.
3. Select completed and save.

Signoffs record the authenticated maintenance user's identity and server timestamp. Inspection signoff follows technician signoff and must be by a different person. Step, prerequisite-card and functional-test checks are no longer part of the simplified completion flow. These are recorded account attestations, not approvals for return to service ([ADR-0004](adr/0004-logbook-generator-and-status-record-system.md)).

Inspection signoff is the signature of the person who may approve the work under 14 CFR 43.7, so it requires a numbered maintenance certificate on the signer's profile: a **Certified mechanic** record with a certificate number under **My certifications**, issued on or before today (with or without Airframe, Powerplant or IA selected), or, on older profiles that predate My certifications, a license number with a certificate type naming A&P, airframe, powerplant, mechanic, IA (inspection authorization) or repairman. A pilot certificate alone does not qualify, and neither do other types such as AME or CRS. Without any certificate on file, the **Inspection signoff** button is disabled with the hint "Add your certificate type and number under My certifications to sign as inspector."; with only a pilot or other certificate, the hint is "Add a mechanic, repairman or inspection authorization certificate under My certifications to sign as inspector." The server refuses the signoff with the same messages. Logbook export's certificate lookup is unchanged. Technician signoff needs no certificate, because 14 CFR 43.3(d) lets supervised uncertificated workers perform the work. Signoffs recorded before this rule stay as they are.

Save content changes before signing. Content edits or new evidence invalidate existing signatures; status-only changes retain them, except a change back to Draft, which clears them because a draft is not ready to be signed. A cleared signoff stays in the card's change history, in the "before" of the save that cleared it.

The job-card lists and the card editor offer the same signoffs, exactly those the server accepts (`signoffRefusals`): technician once the card is past draft and has a completion date of today or earlier; inspection after that, to a different person with a maintenance certificate. A signoff already recorded can be recorded again, and a new technician signoff clears the inspection signoff. A signoff the server would refuse is disabled, with the server's reason as the list button's tooltip and beside the editor's button. Completed and canceled cards are read-only. Work orders cannot be completed or canceled until their linked cards are completed or canceled. Work-order lists show requested-work counts separately from detailed task-card counts and include task-card parts delays and originating discrepancies.

Card completion does not automatically advance inspection compliance, resolve a discrepancy or move a component. Complete the parent work order to update its routine inspection compliance; use the existing discrepancy and component screens for resolution or movement. Maintenance access and a connection are required for changes; cached cards remain readable offline. Aircraft/program scoping, version checks and audit history apply to card changes, links, signatures and evidence uploads.
