# Work orders

Open **Work orders → Open work order**, select an aircraft, and enter the scope, supervisor and priority. Leave the number blank to assign the next available `WO-YYYY-NNNN` number within the aircraft’s current Flight Program, or enter your own unique number.

Opening an order captures its recording timestamp, aircraft registration, make/model, serial, Hobbs, tach, total time and cycles from the current aircraft record. Select installed components to include their TSN, TSO, TSR and cycles in the header. Unknown overhaul/repair readings remain unrecorded. Aircraft readings and retained component readings remain unchanged when subsequent flights update the aircraft. Components added to the header later capture their current readings with a separate recording timestamp.

Add multiple requested-work rows from the order’s item selector or use **Add to work order** on a discrepancy, inspection or installed component’s service requirement. The picker only offers open orders for that aircraft that do not already contain the item. Free-text requested work is also supported. Source descriptions are captured when added. Associated task templates are copied into independent draft job cards, including their instructions and planning data but no results or signatures. Each requested-work item displays its job cards underneath it, with status and required signoff progress. Click a card to enter results or sign it. The order-level **Job cards** button still provides the full list. Existing orders can use **Add missing task templates**; existing copies are retained without duplication. Existing tasks can be cancelled, but cannot be deleted or relinked to a different source.

On connected deployments, template copying runs in resumable background batches. The inspection dialog and work-order list show **Preparing job cards: X of Y**. You can close the dialog or disconnect while preparation continues. The order is protected from edits and closeout until preparation finishes. If preparation stops, choose **Resume preparation**; already copied cards remain intact. Template contents and versions are captured when the request is accepted, so later template edits do not change the prepared cards. New copies receive unique `TC-<preparation UUID>-<sequence>` numbers. Demo mode continues to copy synchronously.

A requested-work item with associated job cards can only be marked completed after every card is completed with all required signoffs. Cards on another order cannot satisfy this check.

Use **Move up** and **Move down** on requested-work tasks to change their order, then choose **Save work order** (or **Open work order** for a new order). Task details and linked job cards stay with their task. Closed orders cannot be reordered.

Track each task as requested, in progress, delayed for parts, completed or cancelled, with work notes and parts-delay details. The list displays completed/total task counts, linked discrepancies that remain open in the discrepancy register, and the number of tasks delayed for parts. Order status is controlled independently. Marking an individual task complete does not update compliance. Completing the whole work order records compliance for its completed inspections and routine component inspection requirements. Discrepancy resolution remains separate. Component repair and overhaul selections are applied when closing the order.

Upload maintenance records as PDF, JPEG or PNG attachments, up to 7 MiB each, after opening the order. Attachments remain available with closed orders.

To complete an order, complete or cancel every task and supply a completion date, release outcome and final review. Confirm the completion aircraft total time, Hobbs, tach and cycles. Readings must fall between the opening snapshot and current totals; backdated closeout requires explicit readings. Completion cannot predate a completed job card.

Closeout saves an immutable snapshot of completion readings, serviced component identifiers and tracking totals, work performed, completing user and their saved license details. Inspection compliance and the closeout commit atomically. Routine intervals advance from the recorded compliance; existing absolute component limits stay fixed. Cancelled tasks are excluded. One-time inspections with no recurring interval follow the existing archive behavior. Component repair and overhaul counters reset only when explicitly selected in closeout. Lifetime usage, aircraft operational status and discrepancy resolution are not reset.

For a completed order, open **Details → Generate logbook entry**, select a format, enter your license/certificate number and type, review the editable text, then choose **Save as PDF** or **Copy text**. An approved certificate from **My certifications** fills in the number and type, and **Use saved certification** offers each one for the entry's date; a certificate you file or change waits for an administrator's approval before it appears here ([user certifications](user-certifications.md)). The text includes recorded work, parts, references, completion readings, serviced component data and next-due values. It uses the recorded release outcome and leaves a signature line for the certifying person; exporting is not a signature. Historical closed orders without a closeout snapshot show their opening readings with a review note and do not trigger retroactive compliance changes.

Completed and cancelled orders are read-only and excluded from the list unless **Include closed work orders** is selected. The list's search matches the work order number, aircraft registration, supervisor, scope and requested-work descriptions; the counts above it follow the aircraft select only. Work-order completion does not change the aircraft’s operational status.

Maintenance permission and an online connection are required to create or change an order or upload an attachment. Readable orders are scoped to the aircraft’s current Flight Program and aircraft access. Concurrent edits are rejected with a request to reopen the order. Saved orders and attachments persist in the demo cache; connected deployments store orders and file references in Convex, with files in Convex storage. Downloads require a connection outside demo mode.

Closed orders retain a view-only requested-work list with nested job cards that can still be opened. Saving a completed or cancelled status shows a permanent-close warning and confirmation; dismissing it leaves the order unsaved and editable.

## Component repair and overhaul at closeout

**Component work performed** appears below Requested work as soon as a component is selected in the header or a component service/inspection is added. Choices can be saved on an open order, including an unfinished work description. When completing an order, the list includes header components and components linked to completed work. Make an explicit selection for each: **Inspected / serviced only**, **Repaired**, **Overhauled**, or **Repaired and overhauled**. No selection is automatic. Repair and overhaul require a work description; a supporting document/reference can also be recorded. The shared completion date and aircraft readings determine component readings at completion. The form shows TSN, cycles, and before/after TSO and TSR; the final close confirmation lists the resets.

Repair resets TSR; overhaul resets TSO; both resets both. Lifetime time, cycles and service days are preserved. Overhaul renews applicable overhaul requirements using their configured relative cycle and calendar intervals. Other requirements retain their remaining time intervals across a counter reset; their previous compliance may be shown as a negative, rebased reading when it predates zero on the new counter. Absolute lifetime limits are not renewed.

Closing saves component movements, updated requirements, the work-order closeout and immutable before/after readings atomically. Every component, service requirement and inspection the component work changes gets its own Change history entry (`component.closeout`, `component.requirement.closeout`, `inspection.closeout`) with the values before and after and the reason "Component work recorded at close-out of work order WO-…". The log book entry includes the component work description, completion date and reference. Subsequent flights accrue from the completion baseline, including flights after a backdated closeout. A changed component version or a later movement requires review before closeout. Closed orders cannot apply the actions again.

## Component calendar display

The Components section accepts **Prior months in service** when creating a component or setting its baseline. Service age and movement history display months, and remaining calendar time displays months without a secondary day count. Service duration uses the existing average-month convention (365.25 / 12); internal day readings are preserved, including when an existing baseline is reopened and saved. Calendar requirement deadlines still use their configured calendar units and exact due dates.
