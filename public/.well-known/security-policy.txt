ANGEL ONE SECURITY POLICY

Angel One keeps aircraft maintenance records for a mission flight program. We
welcome reports of security weaknesses and handle them in confidence.


REPORT A VULNERABILITY

Email accounts@angeloneonline.org with "Security" in the subject. Please don't
report it in a public issue, post or pull request.

Include:

- what is affected (a page, an address or a function) and the weakness;
- the steps to reproduce it, with any proof of concept;
- the impact you expect, and what you were able to reach;
- when you tested;
- how to reach you, and whether you want to be credited.


WHAT TO EXPECT

- We acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
- We triage it within 10 business days: we confirm or decline it and tell you
  the next step.
- We keep you informed until it is fixed, and credit you if you wish. This is
  not a paid bounty programme.


SCOPE

In scope: the web app at https://ops.angeloneaviation.org and its Convex
backend (the functions, sign-in and file storage the app uses).

Out of scope:

- denial of service;
- social engineering of our members, staff or providers;
- physical attacks;
- automated scanning that degrades the service;
- third-party services, such as Convex, Cloudflare, GitHub or Resend:
  report those to the provider.


TESTING RULES

- Test only with accounts you own or were given for testing.
- Do not access, change or delete other users' data. If you reach it by
  accident, stop, keep no copy, and tell us what you saw.
- Use no more data or requests than you need to show the problem.


SAFE HARBOUR

If you follow this policy in good faith, we consider your research authorised,
we will not take legal action against you for it, and we will say so if anyone
else does. Good faith means you keep to the rules above, avoid harm to people's
privacy and to the service, and give us time to fix the problem.


COORDINATED DISCLOSURE

Keep the details private until a fix is released or 90 days have passed since
your report, whichever is sooner. We will agree the publication date with you.
